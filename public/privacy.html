Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Update Bro! ("we," "us," "the site") is a news discovery and aggregation service. We show headlines, source attribution, and short AI-generated summaries, and link every story directly to the original publisher — we do not publish, host accounts, or sell anything on this site. This policy explains the little data we do handle.

What we collect

Local storage (always, essential)

Your browser stores two things on your device, never on our servers:

  • Your light/dark theme choice
  • A cached copy of the homepage, so your next visit paints instantly instead of showing a loading screen

Neither is shared with us or anyone else — it stays on your device until you clear your browser's site data.

Analytics cookies (only if you choose "Accept All")

If you accept analytics cookies in the consent banner, we load Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate usage — which pages are viewed, how long visits last, approximate country-level location, device/browser type, and referring site. This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. We do not use it to individually identify you, and we do not combine it with any other data, because we don't collect any other personal data to combine it with.

If you choose "Necessary Only," Google Analytics is never loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

What we do not collect

There are no accounts, logins, newsletters, forms, or chat widgets on this site. We do not collect your name, email address, payment information, or any other personally identifiable information, because nothing on the site asks for it.

Cookie consent

On your first visit you're shown a choice between "Necessary Only" and "Accept All Cookies." Your choice is remembered in local storage so you're not asked again. To change your mind, clear this site's data in your browser settings (or clear local storage via your browser's developer tools) — you'll see the banner again on your next visit.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics — only loaded after you accept analytics cookies; see above.
  • Google Favicons (google.com/s2/favicons) — used to display each publisher's site icon on story cards. This is a simple icon lookup, always on regardless of your cookie choice, and does not carry any information about you beyond the publisher domain being looked up.
  • The 36 news publishers we link to (TechCrunch, The Verge, BBC, and others) — when you click through to read a full story, you leave this site and that publisher's own privacy policy applies. We have no visibility into or control over what they do with your visit.
  • Vercel, our hosting provider — processes standard web server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) as part of serving the site; see Vercel's privacy policy.

Data retention

We don't retain any personal data ourselves — there's nowhere for it to be stored, since we run no database or user accounts. Local storage on your device persists until you clear it. Google Analytics data (if you opted in) is retained according to Google's own default retention settings.

Your choices

  • Change your cookie choice at any time by clearing this site's local storage in your browser.
  • Use a browser extension (e.g. Google's Analytics Opt-out Add-on) to block Google Analytics outright.
  • Use your browser's built-in tracking protection or a content blocker — nothing on this site requires cookies or analytics to function.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and has no age-gated features.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
